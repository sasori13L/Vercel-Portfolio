    About

    I've spent the last decade working end to end — schema design and APIs through to the interface and the deploy. Most of it has been in PHP and Vue.js, but the stack has widened with the work: Flutter for offline-first mobile, WordPress and Shopify for client sites, Next.js wherever a headless frontend made more sense than a monolith.

    Lately most of my work sits where two things meet: wiring OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini into existing modules so they automate what used to be manual, and using Spec-Driven Development to keep that kind of fast-moving feature work from turning into a mess six months later.

    I like the projects other developers avoid — an undocumented codebase, a company still tracking cash flow in spreadsheets, a grading system that used to run entirely on paper. There's usually a clear before and after, and I'd rather be measured on that than on lines of code.
